I studied mathematics and started my career teaching it. Both still shape how I work: break the problem down, find the real cause, back the fix with data, and explain it so everyone understands.

I then spent five and a half years at CLIQ Digital in Amsterdam, growing from a country-operations internship into owning payment operations. I helped build a card-payments capability from scratch, ran subscription products from the UK to LATAM, led a team of three Product Operations specialists, and took ownership whenever authorization dropped, fraud spiked or a partner integration stalled.

What I bring is the bridge between both sides. I know the payment flow well enough to discuss parameters and error codes with engineers and PSPs, and the business well enough to advise merchants on their performance and tell management what a problem costs and what we're doing about it. Lately I've been building small AI agents to take repetitive operational work off people's plates.

Next, I'm looking for a team where payments, product thinking and operational improvement meet, and where getting to the root cause is valued.
